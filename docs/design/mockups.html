Intent

The fastest path from "I know the number" to a ringing call. The number field is focused whenever the window is key, so typing digits works without clicking.

Interaction

  • Keys on screen and the keyboard both type into the field; ⌫ deletes, ↩ calls, long-press 0 gives +.
  • The account picker is a compact pop-up button at the left of its own row above the number field (as in Acrobits Groundwire). It sets the outgoing account for this call only; its default comes from "Default for outgoing". The dot shows that account's registration.
  • Call and ↩ dial exactly what is typed: a number that matches nothing in Contacts or the Call Log is called as entered, no suggestion needed. On the Contacts and Call Log tabs ↩ in the field does the same.
  • Pasting sip: or tel: URIs and numbers with spaces or dashes is accepted; the dial string is normalised on call.
  • Status bar: one item per enabled account. A failed account shows the short reason in red; clicking an item opens that account in Settings.

Account pop-up

  • Lists every account. Clicking a name picks the outgoing account (checkmark) and closes the menu.
  • The switch at the right turns the account on or off without closing the menu. Off means no registration and no outgoing calls; the account stays in the list, dimmed, so it can be turned back on, and it leaves the status bar. A failing account (Backup: 403) is silenced in two clicks.
  • The same state is the "Enabled" switch in the account editor (screen 9) and the Disabled status in Settings › Accounts.

Status bar · network states

  • No network: a grey dot and "No network" at the left; the account items are hidden until the Mac is back online.
  • Reconnecting…: each account that is re-registering shows an orange dot and its retry countdown ("Office · retry in 8 s"). Every main-window mock shares this bar.

Decided

  1. Q1.1Decided: letters typed in the number field show contact suggestions in a dropdown under the field.
  2. Q1.2Decided: an empty field + Call redials the last number.

Decided

  1. Q1.3Decided (2026-10-12): A. Accounts are switched on and off in the account pop-up; the status bar has no menu and shows enabled accounts only.
  2. Q1.4Decided: without a network the status bar shows only "No network" with a grey dot.
  3. Q1.5Decided: while reconnecting, each account shows an orange dot and "retry in N s".

Intent

Same window, same size as when idle, as in MicroSIP. During a call the Dialpad tab becomes the call panel, and Contacts and Call Log stay one click away for looking up a transfer target.

Interaction

  • The window keeps its 340 × 520 pt size. On the Dialpad tab the call panel replaces the keypad; when the last call ends the dialpad comes back.
  • Mute, Hold and Record are toggles: filled when on. Transfer opens a popover with contact suggestions: Transfer Now (blind) and Call First (attended, see screen 5).
  • Keypad swaps the panel for a DTMF pad with Back to call. Digit keys on the keyboard also send DTMF while the window is key; sent digits echo above the pad.
  • On Contacts or Call Log a one-line bar above the list shows the call (name, timer, mute, end). Clicking the bar returns to the Dialpad tab.
  • The number field stays live: typing a number and ↩ places a second call and holds this one.
  • Chip row shows only what carries information: via Account only when two or more accounts are enabled; the SRTP lock only when the media is actually encrypted (never a "no SRTP" chip); the codec chip with the quality bars; the speaker icon last. With one account and no SRTP the row is the codec chip and the speaker.
  • Quality chip: codec + 4 signal bars (see the quality and network states below). ⌘D ends the call, ⌘⇧M toggles mute while MacroSIP is the frontmost app.
  • While recording, a red REC pill with the elapsed time sits in the state line of the call panel (Connected · REC 01:37) and shows as a small chip in the summary bar on the other tabs. Merge is disabled with one call and turns on when a second call is connected.

Quality, volume and network states

  • Quality bars (0–4) show measured call quality: packet loss, jitter and round-trip time from RTCP, combined into an estimated MOS. Hovering the codec chip shows a tooltip with the four numbers.
  • Poor connection: 1 bar, and a yellow banner under the chips names the cause ("Poor connection: 12 % packet loss"). It goes away when quality recovers; the call is not interrupted.
  • Volume: the speaker button next to the codec chip opens a popover with Speaker and Microphone sliders and a Reset link. ⌘↑ / ⌘↓ change the speaker volume while MacroSIP is the frontmost app.
  • Reconnecting…: the state line turns orange and counts the seconds ("Reconnecting… 4 s"). The call timer keeps running and every control stays enabled. The status bar shows the reconnecting accounts (screen 1).

Decided

  1. Q4.1Decided: the window does not grow; the call panel replaces the dialpad area (shown here).
  2. Q4.2Decided: no volume or mic level controls in the call panel. Replaced by Q4.6.
  3. Q4.3Decided: no global hotkeys in v1.
  4. Q4.4Decided: the bars on the codec chip show measured quality; hover shows loss, jitter, RTT and MOS.
  5. Q4.5Decided: a poor connection shows 1 bar and a yellow banner with the cause under the chips.
  6. Q4.6Decided: a speaker button next to the codec chip opens a volume popover (Speaker, Microphone, Reset); ⌘↑ / ⌘↓ change the speaker volume.
  7. Q4.7Decided: while a call reconnects, the timer keeps running, the state reads "Reconnecting… N s" in orange and the controls stay enabled.

Intent

Handle a second call without losing the first, inside the same 520 pt window. Exactly one call is active; every other call is held, and the row list makes that visible at a glance.

Interaction

  • On the Dialpad tab, call rows sit at the top of the panel and the buttons below act on the active call. Up to 4 rows fit; a fifth call is rejected with 486 Busy.
  • Clicking a held row (or Resume) makes it active and holds the current one. Each row has its own small red end button.
  • On Contacts or Call Log the summary bar shows the active call plus "+N on hold"; clicking it returns to the panel.
  • A new incoming call while on a call follows screen 6; Answer holds the current call.
  • Attended transfer: Transfer → Call First holds the original call and calls the target. The banner completes or cancels the transfer.
  • Merge joins all connected calls into a local conference (mixed on this Mac). Split takes one participant back out as a held call. Ending the conference ends every leg.
  • Reconnecting…: a call that is reconnecting keeps its row; the state reads "Reconnecting… 4 s" in orange before the running timer, its bars go empty, and Resume and End stay available.

Decided

  1. Q5.1Decided: at most 4 simultaneous calls.
  2. Q5.2Decided: Merge auto-including held calls comes later.
  3. Q5.3Decided: a reconnecting call keeps its row and timer, with "Reconnecting… N s" in orange.

Intent

An incoming call must be impossible to miss and quick to act on, wherever you are working, without stealing keyboard focus from the app you are typing in.

Interaction

  • Shown only when the main window is hidden or behind another app; if MacroSIP is frontmost the incoming call appears inside the main window instead.
  • Non-activating floating panel on all Spaces, centred on the screen with the pointer; it can be dragged and remembers its position.
  • Shortcuts while it is visible: ⌘↩ answer, ⌘. decline. Esc only silences the ringtone.
  • The notification carries Answer / Decline actions, so the call can also be handled from Notification Center or a locked screen. Both disappear when the call is answered, declined or cancelled by the caller.
  • Decline sends 486 Busy Here. An unanswered call becomes a missed call (badge on Dock, menu bar, Call Log tab).
  • Countdown ring "Auto-answer in N s" with Cancel when auto-answer applies (mode All, or Contacts only and the caller is a contact). A server answer-after header shows the same strip.

Decided

  1. Q6.1Decided: the floating window appears only when the main window is not visible or not frontmost; otherwise the call shows inside the main window.
  2. Q6.2Decided: notifications use the alert style (they stay until acted on).

Intent

Standard macOS settings window: toolbar tabs, grouped forms, changes apply immediately (no Save button), window title follows the selected tab.

General

  • "Make Default" registers MacroSIP for sip:, sips: and tel: through Launch Services; the line under it says which app currently owns the scheme.
  • Language switches the app's own language (macOS per-app language) and asks to restart.
  • Order: toggles, Do Not Disturb, Language, Updates, then Advanced, a disclosure group closed by default (dimmed here) with Links and Troubleshooting. The call settings are in the Calls tab and "Sort contacts by" in Directory (screen 13), so the pane fits a 13-inch screen with Advanced closed.

Accounts

  • Not in the brief: the account editor needs a home. This pane lists accounts with live status; +, −, Edit… and double-click open the editor sheet (screen 9).
  • Status shows Disabled for an account that is switched off (in the account pop-up on the main window or with "Enabled" in the editor). An enabled account with "Register with server" off shows Outgoing only.

Audio

  • The level meter is live while this pane is open. Audio test: Test plays two notes for 2 seconds on the speaker device, next to the microphone meter.
  • Hold music: Built-in melody, a custom file (converted once by the engine) or Off; the far end hears it while on hold. A file that cannot be read keeps the previous source and the footer says why.
  • Sound events: short beeps on the speaker for connect/end, dial pad keys outside a call, new voicemail and registration or network errors (at most once per 10 s); all off by default, never heard by the other side.
  • Headset buttons: HID telephony buttons of a USB headset answer, hang up, hold, mute and redial; the row names the connected headset.
  • Device lists update when devices are plugged in or out.
  • Ringtone volume sets the ringtone level only; call audio is set in the call panel's volume popover (screen 4).
  • Switch to a headset: a newly connected headset takes over the microphone and speaker; removing it returns to the previous devices.
  • Voice processing is an information row, not a control: it says which processing is in use for the current devices.

Network

  • Transport and SRTP are not here: each account sets its own in the account editor (screen 9). Echo cancellation is in Audio.
  • RTP port range (start and count; at least 8 ports, nothing past 65535; an invalid value outlines the field red and reverts on blur) and rport; DNS SRV with an optional nameserver, enabled only while SRV is on. rport applies at once; the port range and DNS need an engine restart: an orange banner "Network changes are waiting for a restart of the engine. Accounts register again." with Restart now appears above the groups, disabled during a call ("End your calls first.").

Codecs

  • Order sets SDP preference. Bitrate column is informational.
  • Opus group: Packet-loss correction (FEC) on and Silence suppression (DTX) off by default. Both apply only to calls that use Opus.

  • Reject calls from is applied by the engine before anything rings: 603 Decline, or 433 for anonymous callers. "Numbers not in Contacts" compares with every number of every contact. The call log gets a missed entry "Rejected: rule".
  • The incoming window shows a countdown and Cancel whenever auto-answer will fire (screen 6).

Intent

Up to 12 keys for the people and codes used all day, with each person's line state at a glance, like the key extension module next to a desk phone.

What a key does

  • Call (handset): dials the number from the outgoing account. In a call, the current call goes on hold and the new one starts (screen 5).
  • DTMF (#): in a call, sends the digits; when idle, dials them as a number, so "Pickup *8" works as a key.
  • Blind transfer (⇄): transfers the active call to the number at once.
  • Attended transfer (⇄): holds the active call and calls the number; Complete Transfer appears in the call panel.
  • Add to conference (people): calls the number and joins it to the active call when answered.
  • Transfer and conference keys are dimmed with no call (Sales queue, Conference room in A).
  • Smart types (two glyphs): call when idle, act on the active call when in one. Blind and attended share the ⇄ glyph; the tooltip names the type.

BLF

  • Dot: green available, red on the phone, orange blinking ringing, grey no presence information. A key with BLF off has no dot.
  • Same subscription as contacts with "Watch presence": a key and a contact with the same number share one subscription.
  • Click a ringing key (orange tint, "ringing") to pick the call up: MacroSIP dials the pickup code with the key's number.
  • Hover shows name, number, state, type and shortcut.

Panel

  • The gear opens Settings › Quick keys. An empty dashed slot opens it with a new row. Right-click a key: Edit…, Remove.
  • Bottom: the window grows by about 110 pt; the strip shows two rows of three and scrolls for the rest. To save room it shows only the dot and the name; the tooltip gives the number and type.
